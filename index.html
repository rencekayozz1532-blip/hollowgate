How to play

Goal. Find 4 fuses and a fuel can, bring them to the generator shed, start it, then run out through the gate. Follow the arrow and the green beam. Crank the gate to open it faster, but cranking is loud. Carrying supplies slows you and makes you easier to notice. When it is chasing you, sprinting wears you out more slowly and you recover faster, and even out of breath you can still stumble along.

The Floorbound hunts by sight and sound. One touch and you are dead. Running, doors and the generator are loud. Crouching is quiet. Your flashlight shows you the way and shows you to it, but holding its beam on a chasing Floorbound makes it recoil for a moment. That costs battery and needs about 7 seconds to recharge. Two rare blue shock charges hide in each run: face it from up close and tap Zap (R on a keyboard) and it collapses for about 20 seconds, then gets up angry and searching.

Hide in lockers when it loses sight of you. While you are inside, your map becomes a tracker showing where the Floorbound is and which way it is heading (tap the map to enlarge it), so you know when it is safe to come out. Throw bottles to pull it away. Close doors to slow it. The NSE bar shows how loud you are. Once per run, after the second fuse, the Floorbound hunts you for about half a minute. Hide in a locker to break it. Beware: some survivors are not real. Real teammates show on your map. Hold your flashlight on a fake for two seconds to expose it.

Touch. Left thumb moves (hold or tap the sprint button, or push the stick past its ring, to run). Right thumb looks. Tap USE near glowing objects.

Keyboard. WASD move, Shift run, C crouch, F light, E use, Q throw, mouse drag to look, Esc pause.
