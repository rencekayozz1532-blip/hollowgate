How to play

Goal. Find 4 fuses, carry them to the generator shed, start it, then run out through the gate before the Warden catches you.

The Warden hunts by sight and sound. Running, doors and the generator are loud. Crouching is quiet. Your flashlight shows you the way and shows you to it.

Hide in lockers when it loses sight of you. Throw bottles to pull it away.

Touch. Left thumb moves (tap the sprint button to run). Right thumb looks. Tap USE near glowing objects.

Keyboard. WASD move, Shift run, C crouch, F light, E use, Q throw, H bandage, mouse drag to look, Esc pause.
